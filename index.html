THE PURPOSE

Make the reasoning behind public decisions public.

Voting records show the outcome of a decision. This proposal would also make it easier for citizens to understand the reasoning, evidence, anticipated impacts, and alternatives behind that decision.

“

An elected representative’s responsibility should extend beyond casting a vote. Citizens should have access to the reasoning, evidence, and anticipated consequences behind decisions made on their behalf.

THE PROPOSAL

Six core provisions

Designed to improve transparency without dictating how any official must vote.

01

Explain votes in advance

Brief intended-position statements before scheduled votes when sufficient notice is available.

02

Provide evidence afterward

Fuller explanations within seven calendar days, with relevant statistics, sources, and acknowledged uncertainties.

03

Address constituent impact

Explain anticipated effects on residents of the represented state or congressional district.

04

Cover major amendments

Apply disclosures to significant amendments affecting spending, rights, responsibilities, or a bill’s purpose.

05

Allow emergency exceptions

Permit documented exceptions to advance notice while retaining post-vote explanations.

06

Explain presidential vetoes

Request fuller public veto explanations identifying objections, evidence, and possible legislative alternatives.
